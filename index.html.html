About Arbor

Where technology meets nature.

Arbor Smart Care is an innovative plant-tech startup built on a simple belief: caring for plants should be joyful — not a chore. Founded by Arnav and Vrushabh, we combine intelligent automation with beautiful design to make plant parenthood effortless for everyone.

From forgetful beginners to passionate plant collectors, Arbor gives every leaf the precise care it deserves — using smart sensors, automated watering, and thoughtful control.
